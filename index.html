01 / About

A little about
how I work.

I’m interested in engineering that is useful, understandable and grounded in what the hardware can actually do.

I’m a student based in Kollam, Kerala, India, with interests spanning computer science, electronics, embedded systems, robotics and software development.

I like working across the full build process: understanding a problem, choosing practical components, connecting hardware and software, testing behaviour, and documenting what needs improvement.

My approach is iterative. I value working prototypes, honest progress reporting and solutions that respect real constraints such as cost, reliability and available hardware.

06 / Behind the lens

Photography.

A creative practice built around observation, composition, light and the small details that are easy to miss.

I’m developing my photography through experimentation with phone cameras, computational photography and mobile editing. I have worked with a Samsung Galaxy A70 and GCam, and use Lightroom Mobile to shape images toward my own style.

I’m especially interested in close-up details, nature, textures, light and composition. I’m also exploring dedicated cameras and interchangeable-lens systems, balancing image quality, handling, viewfinders, video and affordable lenses.

My aim is to keep developing a recognisable style while learning to make more deliberate decisions in-camera and during editing.

Current creative toolkit

Capture · Edit · Refine

Capture: Samsung Galaxy A70, GCam and continued exploration of dedicated cameras.

Editing: Lightroom Mobile, using a high-contrast, detail-focused starting point with personal adjustments.

Interests: Nature, macro-style close-ups, textures, water droplets and creative composition.
